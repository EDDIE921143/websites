## Matching video demonstrations

Five video links use Your Move's free exercise clips: https://ymove.app/free-exercise-videos . Its published terms allow use inside apps, with no sign-up or payment; clips may not be resold as a standalone library. Your Move is credited in the player. Clips download to the app cache only when requested, so replay can work offline. Gym settings can clear this cache without touching workouts. Matches cover pec-deck fly, reverse fly, leg extension, cable tricep pushdown and hammer curl; unmatched exercises keep still reference positions rather than an unrelated video.
